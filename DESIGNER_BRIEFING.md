# ABOUT SOULWARE

## Brand Mission (DO NOT CHANGE THIS)

Soulware crafts AI-powered solutions that unlock human potential and operational excellence, bringing the power of AI within reach of every organization.

We analyze where time and talent are lost, and build tools that integrate seamlessly into your workflows. Automating the repetitive, enhancing decisions, and empowering your people to focus on what matters most. The result: smarter work, greater impact, and a workplace where technology amplifies human potential.

## Brand Personality

- **The Creator:** Innovation, building solutions, mastery, transformation
- **The Everyman:** Approachable, relatable, accessible, authentic

# VISUAL DIRECTION: THE WORKSHOP

## Core Concept

Show humans as skilled craftspeople in beautiful, futuristic workshop environments. Think:
- Tony Stark's workshop (personal, high-tech) + Apple's clean modernism
- Warm wood tones + cool holographic tech accents
- Artisan craft precision + digital sophistication
- 70% warm and human / 25% cool and tech / 5% energy and action

## Visual References

**Films/Shows to Study:**
- **Her (2013)** - Warm futuristic aesthetic (PRIMARY REFERENCE)
- **Iron Man series** - Tony's workshop, human + tech
- **Oblivion (2013)** - Clean futuristic interiors (add more warmth)

**Architecture/Spaces:**
- Apple Park - Modern workspace with natural light
- Google/IDEO offices - Workshop-like collaborative spaces
- Dyson headquarters - Engineering + craft visible

**Photography Style:**
- Apple "Shot on iPhone" campaigns - Authentic, diverse, high quality

## Logo Specifications

### Variations Required:

**1. Full Lockup**
- Icon + "SOULWARE" wordmark
- Minimum width: 150px digital, 1.5" print
- Use: Website header, presentations, official docs

**2. Icon + Wordmark (No Tagline)**
- Most commonly used version
- Minimum width: 120px digital, 1.25" print

**3. Icon Only**
- Perfect square
- Minimum: 32px digital, 0.5" print
- Use: Social media avatars, app icon, favicon

**4. Wordmark Only**
- Just "SOULWARE" text
- Use: Text-heavy contexts, tight horizontal spaces

**5. Reverse (Light Version)**
- White/light colors for dark backgrounds
- All variations

**6. Monochrome**
- Single color version (any brand color)
- Use: Single-color printing, embroidery

### Color Treatments Required:

**A. Primary (Warm Mono):**
- Icon: Honey Wood (#C4A27A)
- Text: Deep Espresso (#2C2520)

**B. Tech Accent (Gradient):**
- Icon: Gradient Honey Wood → Bright Cyan
- Shows soul (warm) → ware (cool) fusion

**C. Reverse:**
- All elements: Workshop White (#F5F1E8)

**D. Monochrome:**
- Single color version in each brand color

### Typography in Logo:

**Wordmark "SOULWARE":**
- Font recommendation: Neue Haas Grotesk Medium OR similar
- Characteristics: Clean, modern, confident, warm
- Letterspacing: +50 to +75 (breathing room)
- Weight: Medium to Bold
- Always uppercase

**Requirements:**
- Must be legible at small sizes
- Must work in all color variations
- Should feel crafted, not generic
- Reflects both precision AND warmth

# DELIVERABLE 4: PHOTOGRAPHY & IMAGERY

## Photography Art Direction

### What to Show:

**Humans (Primary Focus):**
- Diverse professionals (age, ethnicity, gender, ability)
- Modern business casual clothing (Everlane/Uniqlo aesthetic)
- Authentic work moments (analyzing, building, collaborating)
- Confident, focused, capable expressions
- Must be REAL PEOPLE, not stock photos

**Environments (Context):**
- Modern workshop-style workspaces
- Warm materials: wood, natural light, plants
- Clean and organized (not cluttered)
- Futuristic but approachable
- Mix of individual focus areas and collaborative spaces

**Technology (Accent):**
- Holographic UI overlays (added in post-production)
- Clean modern hardware (tablets, minimal displays)
- Represents 25% of composition max
- Always supporting, never overwhelming human

### Lighting Specifications:

**Primary (70%):** Warm, natural window light OR warm artificial (3000-3500K)
**Accent (25%):** Cool screen glow, holographic cyan (added in post)
**Overall:** Warm and inviting, with depth from shadows

### Technical Specs:

**Camera:**
- Minimum 4000px width, RAW format
- 35mm-85mm focal length
- f/2.8-f/5.6 aperture (environmental context, subject separation)

**Post-Production:**
- Warm color grading (3800-4500K feel)
- Add holographic UI overlays (Bright Cyan color)
- Maintain 70% warm / 25% cool balance
- Natural skin tones preserved

## Your Task:

**Create:**
- Photography style guide with visual examples
- Shot list template for future photoshoots
- Post-production specifications for holograms
- Holographic UI design templates

**Provide Examples:**
- 3-5 photo composites showing final style
- Before/after showing post-production
- Various scenarios (analyze, build, collaborate)
