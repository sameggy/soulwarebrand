Soulware crafts AI-powered solutions that unlock human potential and operational excellence, bringing the power of AI within reach of every organization.

The result: smarter work, greater impact, and a workplace where technology amplifies human potential.

The Workshop aesthetic brings this mission to life by showing humans as skilled craftspeople in futuristic workshop environments, using advanced AI as their tools.

## Logo System

### Primary Mark: The Soulware Compass

**Design Concept:**
A geometric precision compass (artisan tool) fused with data nodes (AI technology). Represents both craft mastery and technological precision.

**Components:**
- Circular compass/divider tool (outer ring)
- Cardinal axis lines (precision arms)
- Tech data nodes at key points (AI integration)
- Center point (human at center of system)
- Secondary diagonal precision lines (mastery details)

**Logo Variations Needed:**

1. **Full Lockup**
   - Icon + "SOULWARE" wordmark
   - Use for: Website header, presentations, official documents
   - Minimum size: 150px wide (digital), 1.5" (print)

2. **Icon Only**
   - Just the compass symbol
   - Use for: Social media avatars, app icon, small spaces
   - Minimum size: 32px (digital), 0.5" (print)

3. **Reverse (Light)**
   - White version for dark backgrounds
   - Use for: Dark website sections, video overlays

4. **Monochrome**
   - Single color version (any brand color)
   - Use for: Single-color printing, embroidery, etching

- **70% Warm colors** (human, soul, craft)

## Typography System

### Primary Typeface: NEUE HAAS GROTESK

**Why This Font:**
- Swiss precision (systematic, reliable)
- Warm and approachable (not cold or robotic)
- Excellent legibility at all sizes
- Modern without being trendy
- Professional but accessible

**Alternatives If Unavailable:**
- GT America
- Inter (free, excellent web font)
- Helvetica Neue (system fallback)

**Weights to Use:**
- Light (300) - Taglines, supporting text, captions
- Regular (400) - Body copy, standard text
- Medium (500) - Subheadings, emphasis, buttons
- Bold (700) - Headlines, major impact

### Typography Hierarchy

**Display / H1 - Major Headlines**
- Font: Neue Haas Grotesk Bold
- Size: 48-72px (digital) / 36-48pt (print)
- Line Height: 1.1 (tight, impactful)
- Letter Spacing: -0.5% (slightly condensed)
- Color: Deep Espresso (#2C2520)
- Use: Hero headlines, page titles, major statements

**H2 - Section Headlines**
- Font: Neue Haas Grotesk Medium
- Size: 32-40px (digital) / 24-32pt (print)
- Line Height: 1.2
- Letter Spacing: 0%
- Color: Deep Espresso (#2C2520)
- Use: Section headers, feature titles

**H3 - Subsection Headers**
- Font: Neue Haas Grotesk Medium
- Size: 24-28px (digital) / 18-22pt (print)
- Line Height: 1.3
- Letter Spacing: 0%
- Color: Warm Walnut (#8B7355)
- Use: Subsections, card headers, smaller sections

**H4 - Small Headers / Overlines**
- Font: Neue Haas Grotesk Regular
- Size: 14-16px (digital) / 11-13pt (print)
- Line Height: 1.4
- Letter Spacing: +8-10% (expanded, all caps)
- Color: Warm Walnut (#8B7355)
- Case: UPPERCASE
- Use: Category labels, overlines, eyebrows

**Body Large - Intro/Lead Text**
- Font: Neue Haas Grotesk Regular
- Size: 18-20px (digital) / 13-15pt (print)
- Line Height: 1.6 (generous, readable)
- Letter Spacing: 0%
- Color: Warm Walnut (#8B7355)
- Use: Introduction paragraphs, pull quotes, important body text

**Body Regular - Standard Text**
- Font: Neue Haas Grotesk Regular
- Size: 16px (digital) / 11-12pt (print)
- Line Height: 1.6
- Letter Spacing: 0%
- Color: Warm Walnut (#8B7355)
- Use: Standard body copy, most text

**Body Small - Captions/Fine Print**
- Font: Neue Haas Grotesk Regular or Light
- Size: 14px (digital) / 9-10pt (print)
- Line Height: 1.5
- Letter Spacing: 0%
- Color: Warm Walnut (#8B7355) at 80% opacity
- Use: Captions, footnotes, disclaimers, credits

**Buttons / CTAs**
- Font: Neue Haas Grotesk Medium
- Size: 16-18px
- Line Height: 1
- Letter Spacing: +3-5%
- Case: Sentence case preferred (not all caps)
- Use: All interactive buttons and CTAs

**Monospace / Code (Optional Supporting Font)**
- Font: JetBrains Mono Regular
- Use: Code snippets, technical data, terminal-style text
- Color: Deep Tech Blue (#004D99)

## Photography Style

### The Workshop Environment

**Core Principle:**
Show humans as skilled professionals in beautiful, futuristic workshop environments where they use advanced AI tools with mastery and confidence.

**What to Photograph:**

**1. Modern Workshop Spaces**
- Clean, contemporary workspaces with warm materials
- Wood surfaces, metal accents, glass, natural elements
- Natural window light or warm overhead lighting
- Organized and intentional (not cluttered)
- Plants integrated naturally
- Approachable, achievable, "elevated everyday"

**2. Human Presence (CRITICAL)**
- Diverse professionals (age, ethnicity, gender, ability)
- Modern business casual clothing (not suits, not hoodies)
  - Quality basics: clean button-downs, blazers, dark jeans
  - Brands to reference: Everlane, Uniqlo, Arc'teryx Veilance aesthetic
  - Neutral colors: black, white, gray, navy, earth tones
- Authentic work moments (not posed stock photos)
- Focused, confident, capable expressions
- Active engagement with tools/data/systems

**3. What They're Doing:**
- **Analyzing:** Examining holographic data visualizations
- **Building:** Configuring tools, creating solutions
- **Integrating:** Testing how systems work together
- **Collaborating:** Multiple people working together
- **Mastering:** Standing confidently, overseeing operations

**4. Technology Integration:**
- Holographic interfaces floating in workspace (added in post-production)
- Clean, modern hardware (tablets, minimal displays)
- Seamless blend of physical workspace and digital overlays
- AI tools visible but not overwhelming (25% of composition)

### Lighting Specifications

**Primary Lighting (Warm - 70%):**
- Natural window light (best option)
- Warm white artificial (3000-3500K)
- Soft, diffused quality (not harsh)
- Creates depth with gentle shadows

**Accent Lighting (Cool - 25%):**
- Screen/monitor glow (blue-ish tint)
- Holographic UI elements (cyan/blue)
- Subtle LED accents
- Added in post-production for holograms

**Key Principle:**
Overall image should feel warm and human, with cool tech accents supporting but not dominating.

### Composition Guidelines

**Framing:**
- Rule of thirds (don't center everything)
- Generous headroom for text overlays
- Multiple depth layers (foreground, subject, background)

**Focus:**
- Subject sharp, background slightly soft (f/2.8-f/5.6)
- Human always the primary focus
- Environmental context visible but supporting

**Scale:**
- Show both detail (close-ups) and context (wide shots)
- Human-scale environments (not intimidating)
- Mix of individual and collaborative scenes

### Photography DON'Ts

❌ Generic stock photos with fake smiles
❌ Sterile, empty environments (too cold)
❌ Sci-fi costumes or lab coats
❌ Overly dramatic/artificial poses
❌ Cluttered, chaotic backgrounds
❌ Flat, even lighting (no depth)
❌ Technology overwhelming the human
❌ All one demographic (must be diverse)

## Holographic UI Elements

### Visual Style

**Structure:**
- Semi-transparent panels (60-80% opacity)
- Geometric shapes (rectangles, circles, clean lines)
- Glowing edges (2-4px glow in Light Cyan Glow #80E8FF)
- Frosted glass effect (subtle blur)

**Colors:**
- Primary: Bright Cyan (#00D9FF)
- Secondary: True Blue (#0088FF)
- Depth/Shadows: Deep Tech Blue (#004D99)
- Highlights: Light Cyan Glow (#80E8FF)
- Text: White (#FFFFFF) or Light Cyan Glow

**Typography on Holograms:**
- Font: Neue Haas Grotesk Light or Regular
- Smaller than primary text (supporting role)
- Monospace (JetBrains Mono) for code/data

**Content Types:**
- Data visualizations (charts, graphs, metrics)
- Dashboards and KPIs
- Process flows and diagrams
- System status displays
- Always semi-transparent, never solid

### Integration Rules

**Placement:**
- Floating 12-24" in front of or above surfaces
- Human-scale (not too small or overwhelmingly large)
- Must be legible but not distracting

**Lighting Effects:**
- Hologram emits light onto nearby surfaces
- Subtle cyan glow on person's face/hands if near
- Surfaces reflect the holographic color slightly

**Proportion:**
- Holograms = 25% of composition maximum
- Human = dominant element (70%)
- Environment = supporting (remaining)

## Graphic Elements & Patterns

### Workshop Grid Pattern

**Design:**
- Subtle blueprint/technical drawing grid
- Light lines: Sandstone (#E8DCC8) at 15-20% opacity
- Spacing: 40px x 40px grid
- Use: Website backgrounds, presentation slides, subtle texture

**Application:**
- Always very subtle (background only)
- Never distracts from content
- Suggests precision and craftsmanship
- Works on Workshop White backgrounds

### Icon System

**Style:**
- Line-based (not solid fills)
- 2px stroke weight at default size
- Rounded corners (2px radius)
- Geometric and precise

**Colors:**
- Default: Warm Walnut (#8B7355)
- Tech icons: Bright Cyan (#00D9FF)
- Active/selected: Honey Wood (#C4A27A)

**Categories Needed:**
- Analysis (magnifying glass, chart, data)
- Building (hammer, wrench, tools)
- Integration (puzzle pieces, connections)
- Automation (refresh, cycle, flow)
- Enhancement (sparkle, upgrade, optimize)
- Empowerment (person, team, growth)

## Production Specifications

### Logo Files Needed

From designer, request:

**Vector Formats:**
- .AI (Adobe Illustrator native)
- .EPS (universal vector)
- .SVG (web vector)
- .PDF (print vector)

**Raster Formats:**
- .PNG (transparent background, multiple sizes: 512px, 1024px, 2048px, 4096px)
- .JPG (white background, same sizes as PNG)

**Variations Required:**
- Full lockup (icon + wordmark)
- Icon only
- Reverse (white/light)
- Monochrome (single color)

### Photography Specifications

**Camera/Technical:**
- Minimum 4000px width
- RAW format (maximum editing flexibility)
- 35mm-85mm focal length
- f/2.8-f/5.6 aperture (environmental context with subject separation)
- Natural/warm lighting (3800-4500K)

**Post-Production:**
- Warm color grading (70% warm tone)
- Add holographic UI overlays (25% cool accents)
- Maintain natural skin tones
- Subtle atmospheric effects (depth)

**Deliverables:**
- 30-50 final edited images
- Multiple crops: 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 9:16
- Both web (sRGB, 72dpi) and print (Adobe RGB, 300dpi)

### Brand Guidelines Document

**Final deliverable should include:**
- Logo usage and clearspace rules
- Complete color palette (hex, RGB, CMYK, Pantone)
- Typography specifications
- Photography style guide with examples
- Application examples (web, print, social)
- Do's and Don'ts with visual examples
- File library and naming conventions

**Format:**
- PDF (40-60 pages)
- High resolution for print reference
- Interactive (clickable table of contents)
- Includes visual examples of everything

## Checklist: Is It On-Brand?

Before publishing any branded material, verify:

- [ ] Uses real workshop photography (not generic stock)?
- [ ] Color balance is 70% warm / 25% cool / 5% energy?
- [ ] Humans are prominent and empowered (not passive)?
- [ ] Logo used correctly (size, clearspace, approved colors)?

- [ ] Feels like "craft" not "mass-produced"?
- [ ] Accessible (contrast ratios meet WCAG standards)?
- [ ] Aligns with brand voice (confident, warm, expert)?
